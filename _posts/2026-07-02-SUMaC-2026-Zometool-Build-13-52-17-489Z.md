To build the model, we will split into six teams:
 * one team will build 25 junctions and 5 feet
 * five teams will build 3 face modules each, for a total of 15

<img alt="teams" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f3050b55-a93f-4250-8405-9e6813ae37b7" />

Once we have the modules ready, we'll assemble the dome from the floor up.
